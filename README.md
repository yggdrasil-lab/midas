# Midas

Mobile-first progressive web app (PWA) framing personal wealth in terms of **Time Horizon (Runway in Weeks)**.

Named after King Midas, this application is the active companion for Area `10-Wealth` within the Yggdrasil ecosystem. Rather than displaying an abstract dollar scoreboard, Midas translates your budget, compounding asset yields, and weekly spending into tangible survival time.

## Key Screens & Features

- **Freedom Clock Hero**: Real-time counter displaying primary financial runway in weeks and years, with partitioned badges for Emergency Cash, Growth Portfolio, and KiwiSaver.
- **Time Machine (Scenario Simulator)**: Interactive client-side sliders allowing instant recalculation of runway weeks under variable spending levels, asset returns, or lump sum additions.
- **Weekly Burn Tracker**: 7-day budget progress bar showing Monday-to-Sunday spending, remaining allowance, and "Time Banked" forward.
- **3-Second Mobile Log**: Tactile touch keypad for on-the-go expense entry with quick category pills and recent merchants.
- **Statement Dropzone**: Drag-and-drop PDF and CSV statement ingestion with extraction preview and closing balance synchronization to Plutus.
